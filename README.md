A complete, execution-only operating kit to make **$500 in 7 days** from zero (no audience, no
budget, 3 hours/night) and systemize it into a faceless service clearing **$10k/month**.

**The play:** a done-for-you faceless short-form content service. You produce scroll-stopping short
videos for businesses & creators using only free AI tools, land founding clients via direct outreach
this week, then convert them into monthly retainers.
