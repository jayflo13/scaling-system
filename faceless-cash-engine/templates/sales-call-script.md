# Sales Call / Closing Script

Works on a 10-min call, a voice note, or all-text DM. Goal: get them to "yes, send the 3 free."
Don't pitch — diagnose, then prescribe.

---

### 1. Frame (10 sec)
> "Cool, thanks for hopping on. Quick — I just want to see if I can actually help, and if not I'll
> tell you straight. Sound good?"

### 2. Diagnose (2–3 questions, let them talk)
- "How are you handling short-form content right now?"
- "What's stopped you from posting consistently?"
- "If you had 20 ready-to-post videos a month landing in a folder, what would that be worth to you?"

*(They'll tell you their pain and their budget. Listen. Take notes.)*

### 3. Prescribe (tie offer to what they said)
> "Got it. So the problem isn't strategy, it's that making the videos eats time you don't have.
> That's literally all I do — faceless short-form, fully edited, ready to post. You approve, you post."

### 4. Risk-reversal close (the money line)
> "Here's how I make this a no-brainer: I'll make your **first 3 videos free** this week. If you
> love them, we do the full batch of 10 for $250. If you don't, you keep them and we part friends.
> Want me to start?"

### 5. On yes → onboard immediately
> "Awesome. I just need 3 quick things to nail your style —" (send `client-intake.md`).
> Set the expectation: "I'll have your first 3 over within [X days]."

### 6. On "let me think" → shrink the ask
> "Totally fair. The free 3 *is* the no-pressure way to think about it — you'll have something real
> to judge instead of deciding blind. Want me to just make those?"

---

### Closing principles
- **Lead with the free 3.** It collapses risk and is the easiest yes you'll ever get.
- **Talk less than them.** Diagnose, don't monologue.
- **Assume the sale.** "When I start..." not "if you want...".
- **Speed.** Send the intake form before the energy fades. Deliver fast.
