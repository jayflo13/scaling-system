### 1 — Myth-bust
**Hook:** "You don't need a $300 facial to fix your skin."
**Script:** You don't need a $300 facial to fix your skin — you need consistency. A great routine
done daily beats one fancy treatment done twice a year. Cleanse, treat, protect. The treatments we
do here *accelerate* results — they don't replace the basics. Start with the basics, then come see us.
**Caption:** Save your money for what actually moves the needle 💧 Save this. #skincare #medspa #skincaretips #glowup #esthetician

### 2 — Educational
**Hook:** "You're applying your skincare in the wrong order."
**Script:** The order matters more than the products. Thinnest to thickest: cleanser, then toner,
then serum, then moisturizer, then SPF in the morning. Put moisturizer before serum and you're
blocking the active ingredients you paid for. Screenshot this and check your shelf tonight.
**Caption:** The $80 serum can't work if it never reaches your skin 🧴 #skincareroutine #medspa #skincare101 #glowingskin

### 3 — Hot take
**Hook:** "Sunscreen is the best anti-aging product. Not Botox."
**Script:** Hot take: sunscreen out-performs every anti-aging product on the market. Up to 90% of
visible aging is sun damage. You can do all the treatments you want — skip SPF and you're undoing
them daily. Wear it every single day, even indoors. It's the cheapest anti-aging you'll ever buy.
**Caption:** The fountain of youth is $15 and SPF 50 ☀️ #antiaging #sunscreen #medspa #skincaretips

### 4 — Behind-the-result
**Hook:** "Here's what one month of consistent treatments actually does."
**Script:** People expect magic in one session — real change is a month of consistency. Smaller
pores, even tone, that lit-from-within glow. It's not one dramatic before-and-after; it's skin that
quietly gets better every week. That's why we build plans, not one-offs. Ready to start yours?
**Caption:** Consistency is the treatment 🌿 DM us "PLAN" to map yours. #skincarejourney #medspa #glowup

### 5 — FAQ
**Hook:** "Does Botox actually hurt? Honest answer."
**Script:** The honest answer: it's a quick pinch, a few seconds per spot, and it's over in ten
minutes. Most clients say it's less than they feared. We numb sensitive areas, and you're back to
your day immediately. The fear is always bigger than the needle. Questions? Drop them below.
**Caption:** The #1 question we get, answered honestly 💉 #botox #medspa #aesthetics #skincare

### 6 — List
**Hook:** "3 treatments worth the money. 2 that aren't."
**Script:** Worth it: a good chemical peel, microneedling for texture, and consistent SPF. Skip:
random gadget facials with no active ingredients, and anything promising permanent results in one
session. Spend on what's backed by science, not by hype. Save this before you book anything.
**Caption:** A med spa telling you what NOT to buy 🤝 #medspa #skincaretips #honestreview #aesthetics

### 7 — Myth-bust
**Hook:** "Drinking water won't hydrate your skin."
**Script:** Sorry — chugging water won't fix dry skin. Hydration on the surface comes from your
barrier, not your water bottle. You need humectants like hyaluronic acid to pull moisture in, and a
moisturizer to seal it. Drink water for your body; use the right products for your face.
**Caption:** The "just drink more water" myth, busted 💦 #skincaremyths #medspa #hydration #skincare

### 8 — Educational
**Hook:** "Chemical peel vs facial — what's the difference?"
**Script:** A facial cleans, hydrates, and relaxes — great maintenance. A chemical peel actually
resurfaces: it removes dead layers to fade scars, sun spots, and fine lines. Facials maintain;
peels transform. Which one you need depends on your goal — and that's exactly what a consult is for.
**Caption:** Know what you're actually booking 📋 #chemicalpeel #facial #medspa #skincare101

### 9 — Authority / story
**Hook:** "We turn clients away. Here's why."
**Script:** We say no more than you'd think. If someone wants filler they don't need, or a treatment
that's wrong for their skin, we tell them. Trust is worth more than one sale. The goal isn't to sell
you everything — it's to get you the result you actually came for. That's the whole job.
**Caption:** Integrity over upsells, every time 🤍 #medspa #aesthetics #skincare #trustedcare
