# 03 — Outreach: Where The Money Comes From

No audience? Fine. You don't wait for buyers — you go to them. This file is your demand engine.

---

## The funnel math (memorize this)

```
40 messages/day  →  ~3–5 replies  →  ~1–2 conversations  →  ~1 call  →  close 1 in 3–4 calls
```

Over 7 days at 40/day = **~280 touches**. That realistically yields **1–2 paying founding clients.**
This is why volume is the whole game. If the numbers feel slow, the answer is always *more sends*,
not a new strategy. Track everything in `templates/outreach-tracker.csv` so you can see it working.

---

## Where to find prospects (free)

1. **Instagram / TikTok hashtag + filter:** Search your niche (e.g. `#onlinecoach`, `#medspa`,
   `#realtorlife`). Open accounts with a real following (1k–100k) but **inconsistent posting** —
   a few Reels then weeks of silence. That gap is your opening.
2. **Google Maps (local):** Search "med spa near [city]", "gym", "dentist". Visit their socials.
   Weak/abandoned content = perfect prospect. Get their email from their site.
3. **Skool / Facebook groups / X:** Coaching and creator communities are full of people drowning
   in "I need to post more but have no time." Engage genuinely, then DM.
4. **Comment sections of big creators:** Other creators commenting there are your peers/prospects —
   smaller accounts who want to grow.
5. **Apollo.io free tier / their website contact:** for businesses, pull a verified email.

**Qualify each prospect (3-second check):** Do they (a) have a product/service to sell, (b) post
short-form inconsistently, (c) have some audience already? If yes → add to tracker.

## The DM script (cold, personalized)

> **[Line 1 — specific compliment about THEIR content]** "Your reel on [specific topic] was
> genuinely good — the hook grabbed me."
>
> **[Line 2 — the gap]** "Noticed you don't post consistently though — totally get it, content is
> a time sink."
>
> **[Line 3 — the offer + risk reversal]** "I run a faceless short-form studio. I'll make you 3
> ready-to-post videos this week, free, no strings. If you like them, we talk about a batch. If
> not, you keep them anyway. Want me to send a sample?"

Short. Personal. Zero risk to them. **Line 1 is everything** — if it's generic, you get ignored.

## The email script (local businesses)

> **Subject:** quick idea for [Business Name]'s Instagram
>
> Hi [Name] — I help [niche] businesses stay consistent on Instagram/TikTok without lifting a
> finger. I make short, ready-to-post videos for you (you just approve and post).
>
> I'd like to make you **3 sample videos for free** this week so you can see the quality — no cost,
> no commitment. If you like them, I do a batch of 10 for $250. If not, keep them.
>
> Want me to put the 3 together? Just reply "yes."
>
> [Your name] — [portfolio link]

## Follow-up sequence (where most closes actually happen)

- **+24h:** "Hey [name] — still happy to make those 3 free samples this week, just say the word 👍"
- **+48h:** "No worries if it's not a fit — should I close this out or do you want me to send one
  sample so you can judge?" *(the "should I close this out?" line reliably gets replies)*
- **+4 days:** "Last nudge — got a slot opening up this week for one more brand. Want it?"

Three follow-ups. Then move on. ~50% of replies come after the first message.

## The objection cheat-sheet

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "How much?" | "Batch of 10 is $250 to start. But let me make 3 free first so you're not buying blind." |
| "I don't have time to manage this." | "That's the point — you do nothing but approve and post. I handle the rest." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Totally. Let me just send the 3 free samples so you've got something real to think about." |
| "I already tried content / it didn't work." | "Most content fails on the hook, not the topic. That's exactly what I specialize in. Let me show you 3 and you judge." |
| "Can you guarantee views?" | "No one honest can guarantee the algorithm. I guarantee quality and consistency — and you only pay if you love the first 3." |

## Day-7 testimonial + referral script (your scale seed)

> "Really glad you're happy with these! Two quick favors: (1) could you send me a sentence I can
> use as a testimonial? (2) Who's one other [niche] person you know who'd want videos like these?
> Happy to make them 3 free samples too and send you a thank-you."

Every happy client should produce a quote and one warm intro. That's how week 1 becomes month 3.
