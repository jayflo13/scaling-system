# 01 — The Offer

A confused buyer never buys. Your offer must be so clear they can repeat it after one sentence.

---

## The one-liner

> "I produce 10 scroll-stopping faceless short videos for your brand every month — fully edited,
> captioned, and ready to post. You just approve and hit publish."

That's it. You remove the #1 pain every business has with social: *making the content.*

---

## Pricing (built to hit $500 in 7 days)

| Tier | What they get | Price | Use |
|------|---------------|-------|-----|
| **Founding (week 1 only)** | First 10 videos | **$250–$300 one-time** | Land 2 clients = $500. Your launch price to get testimonials fast. |
| **Starter retainer** | 12 videos/mo | $500/mo | Convert founding clients into recurring. |
| **Growth retainer** | 20 videos/mo + content strategy | $1,000/mo | Your $10k/mo workhorse tier. |
| **Scale retainer** | 30 videos/mo, multi-platform repurposing | $2,000/mo | High-value clients. |

**Week-1 target:** close **2 founding clients at $250**, or **1 at $500**. Do not over-engineer it.
Price low *this week only* — you're buying testimonials and proof, not maximizing margin yet.

---

## The risk-reversal (this is what closes)

> "I'll make your first 3 videos before you pay a cent. If you don't love them, you owe nothing
> and you keep the videos. If you do, you pay for the batch of 10."

OR, if you want cash up front:

> "100% money-back guarantee. Don't like the first 3 of your 10? Full refund, keep everything."

De-risking the buy is worth more than any discount. A no-brainer offer + a guarantee beats a
slick pitch every time.

---

## Who buys (target these, in order)

1. **Online coaches & course creators** — high margin, understand content ROI, easy to DM, pay
   fast. *Best week-1 target.*
2. **Local service businesses** — med spas, gyms, dentists, real estate agents, restaurants,
   contractors. Find them with weak/inconsistent social presence (= they know they're failing at it).
3. **E-commerce / personal brands** — already value content, want more output.
4. **Faceless niche pages** (finance, motivation, history) that want to grow but can't keep up
   posting.

### The "already bleeding" filter
Best prospects are accounts that **post short-form inconsistently** — a burst, then silence.
That proves: (a) they believe in short-form, (b) they can't sustain it themselves, (c) they have
budget intent. You're not convincing them content matters; you're solving a pain they already feel.

---

## What makes your service "faceless" (yours AND theirs)

- **You** never appear — you operate the pipeline, deliver files, communicate by text.
- **Their** videos can be faceless too: B-roll + bold captions + trending audio + AI voiceover.
  No filming required on their end either — which is exactly why they can't make these themselves
  and why they'll pay you.

---

## Positioning line for your profile / pitch

> "Faceless short-form content, done for you. You post. I handle everything else."

Keep it boring and clear. Clever loses money.
