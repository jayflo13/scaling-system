# 06 — Find What Converts, Lock It In

This is the difference between hustling forever and building a machine. You don't guess what works —
you **measure it, then pour fuel on the winner.** Two trackers: one for sales, one for content.

---

## Tracker A — Outreach (find your money-printing combo)

Log every message in `templates/outreach-tracker.csv`. Columns:

`date, prospect, niche, platform, opener_type, replied(Y/N), call(Y/N), closed(Y/N), $`

After ~80–100 sends you'll have signal. Compute three numbers per **niche**, per **platform**, and
per **opener_type**:

- **Reply rate** = replies ÷ sends
- **Call rate** = calls ÷ replies
- **Close rate** = closes ÷ calls

### The lock-in rule
Find the single combination — e.g. *(online coaches × Instagram DM × "myth-bust compliment opener")* —
with the best **closes per 100 sends.** That's your winner. Next week, send **80% of your outreach
into that exact combo** and cut the losers. This is how 3 hours/night starts producing predictably.

> What gets measured gets scaled. A 6% close-combo beating a 1% combo means 6× the money for the
> same effort. Find it, then refuse to spread yourself thin.

---

## Tracker B — Content performance (drives retention = retainers)

For each video you deliver, after the client posts, log in `templates/content-calendar.csv`:

`client, video, pillar, hook_style, views, saves, shares, comments`

### What to watch
- **Saves & shares > views.** Saves signal value; they predict follower growth and client happiness.
- Find the **hook style + pillar** that consistently earns saves for that niche.
- Make **more of the winners.** A client whose videos perform **renews.** Renewals are your $10k/mo.

### Lock-in rule
Build a "winning formats" note per niche. When you onboard a new client in that niche, start from
the proven formats instead of from scratch. Your edge compounds.

---

## The weekly 20-minute review (every Sunday)

1. Open both trackers.
2. **Outreach:** What's my best niche × platform × opener? Shift next week's volume toward it.
3. **Content:** Which hooks/pillars got the most saves? Templatize them in CapCut.
4. **Pipeline:** How many warm leads carry over? Who needs a follow-up?
5. **Money:** Revenue this week vs last. One number that must trend up: **recurring (retainer) revenue.**

Write 3 bullets: *what worked, what didn't, what I'll do more of.* That's the entire feedback loop
that turns a scramble into a system.

---

## Leading indicators (so you act before the money shows up)

| If this drops… | Do this |
|----------------|---------|
| Sends/day < 30 | You have no future pipeline. Send more *today.* |
| Reply rate < 5% | Fix line 1 — it's generic. Personalize harder. |
| Call→close < 25% | Lead with the free-first-3 guarantee earlier. |
| Client saves trending down | Refresh hooks/pillars before they churn. |

Outreach volume is a leading indicator of money 7–10 days out. Never let it hit zero, even after a win.
