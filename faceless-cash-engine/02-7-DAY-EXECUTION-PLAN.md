# 02 — The 7-Day Execution Plan

3 hours a night. No theory. Do the blocks in order. Check the boxes.

> **Rule:** Outreach is non-negotiable every day from Day 3 on. Editing is fun and feels
> productive; outreach is what pays. When in doubt, send more DMs.

---

## DAY 1 — Setup (get sellable)

- [ ] **Payment (15 min):** Create a free **Stripe Payment Link** or **PayPal.Me** / **Wise**.
      You need one link you can paste into a chat to get paid. Test it with $1 to yourself.
- [ ] **Identity (20 min):** Make a simple brand name + free logo in **Canva**. Create one
      social profile (Instagram or X) for the studio. Bio = your positioning line from `01-THE-OFFER.md`.
- [ ] **Tools (25 min):** Set up free accounts: **CapCut** (editing), **Canva** (graphics/text),
      **Pexels + Pixabay** (free B-roll), **ElevenLabs** (free voiceover tier), **Google Drive**
      (delivery), **Google Sheets** (trackers). Bookmark them all in one folder.
- [ ] **Lead list (60 min):** Open `templates/outreach-tracker.csv`. Find **40 prospects** using
      the methods in `03-OUTREACH-SCRIPTS.md`. Just collect handles/emails tonight — don't message yet.
- [ ] **Read** `04-AI-DELIVERY-PIPELINE.md` end to end so tomorrow you can produce fast.

**End of Day 1:** you can take money, you have a brand, and you have 40 names.

---

## DAY 2 — Build your proof (3 sample videos)

- [ ] **Pick a sample niche (10 min):** Choose ONE niche you'll target (e.g. online fitness coaches).
      Your samples should look like content *for that niche* so prospects see themselves in it.
- [ ] **Produce 3 sample videos (2.5 hrs):** Follow `04-AI-DELIVERY-PIPELINE.md`. Use hooks from
      `05-PROMPT-LIBRARY.md`. These are your portfolio — they do your selling for you.
- [ ] **Build a 1-link portfolio (20 min):** Drop the 3 videos in a public Google Drive folder or
      a free Canva site / Notion page. This is the link you send prospects.

**End of Day 2:** you have proof. You are now indistinguishable from an established studio.

---

## DAY 3 — Launch outreach (the day the money clock starts)

- [ ] **Send 40 personalized DMs/emails (2 hrs):** Use scripts in `03-OUTREACH-SCRIPTS.md`.
      Personalize the first line to each prospect (reference their actual content). Log every send
      in the tracker.
- [ ] **Reply fast (ongoing):** Keep notifications on. Speed of reply ≈ close rate.
- [ ] **Produce 1 more sample (1 hr)** while waiting for replies, to deepen your portfolio.

**Target:** 3–6 conversations started.

---

## DAY 4 — Outreach + first calls

- [ ] **Send 40 more (1.5 hrs).** Volume compounds.
- [ ] **Book & run calls (1 hr):** Use `templates/sales-call-script.md`. Calls can be a 10-min
      voice/Zoom or even all-text DM. Lead with the risk-reversal.
- [ ] **Send your offer + payment link** to anyone warm. Make it easy to say yes today.

---

## DAY 5 — Close (first cash target)

- [ ] **Follow up every warm lead (45 min):** "Still want me to start your first 3 free/this week?"
      Follow-ups close more than first messages. Most money is in the 2nd–4th touch.
- [ ] **Close 1–2 founding clients (target $500).** Send invoice (`templates/invoice-template.md`)
      or payment link. Collect their intake (`templates/client-intake.md`).
- [ ] **Send 30 more DMs** to keep the pipeline alive even after the first close.

**End of Day 5:** realistic first-cash day. If not yet — you're not behind, you're under-volume.
Send more tomorrow.

---

## DAY 6 — Deliver + keep selling

- [ ] **Produce the client's first 3 videos (2 hrs)** and send them for approval. Speed of first
      delivery sets the relationship tone — be fast.
- [ ] **Send 30 DMs (1 hr).** Never stop the pipeline just because you closed one.

---

## DAY 7 — Bank it, prove it, review

- [ ] **Deliver the full first batch.** Get the client to confirm they're happy.
- [ ] **Ask for a testimonial + 1 referral** (script in `03-OUTREACH-SCRIPTS.md`). This is the
      seed of your $10k/mo machine.
- [ ] **Pitch the retainer:** "Want me to keep these coming every month for $500?" Turn one-time
      into recurring.
- [ ] **Review `06-CONVERSION-TRACKER.md`:** which niche, opener, and platform got replies/closes?
      That winning combo is what you scale. **Lock it in.**

**End of Day 7:** $500 booked (or close, with a full pipeline), one happy client, one testimonial,
and hard data on what converts. Now go to `07-SCALE-TO-10K.md`.

---

### If you're behind on Day 5
You almost certainly have an **input problem, not a strategy problem.** Diagnose in this order:
1. Did you send 40/day? (Most people send ~10 and quit.) → Send more.
2. Are you personalizing line 1? Generic = ignored. → Reference their real content.
3. Are you leading with the free-first-3 risk-reversal? → Lead with it.
4. Are you following up 3×? → Follow up.
Fix the input. The output follows.
