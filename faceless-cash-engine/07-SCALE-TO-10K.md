# 07 — Scale to $10k/Month (The Faceless Machine)

Week 1 proves the offer converts. Now you turn a freelance hustle into a faceless operating system.
$10k/mo isn't "work more hours" — it's **recurring revenue + systemized delivery + leverage.**

---

## The math of $10k/mo

| Path | Clients | Price | You need |
|------|---------|-------|----------|
| A | 20 | $500/mo | High volume, lower touch |
| B | **10** | **$1,000/mo** | **The sweet spot** |
| C | 5 | $2,000/mo | Fewer, premium clients |

Target **Path B: 10 retainer clients at $1k.** That's the cleanest faceless $10k. Everything below
serves that goal.

---

## Lever 1 — Convert one-time → recurring (do this first)

One-time sales are a treadmill. The instant a founding client is happy, pitch the retainer:

> "Want me to keep these coming — 20 videos a month, fully managed, for $1k? You'll never think
> about content again."

**Recurring revenue is the whole game.** 10 retainers = predictable $10k that doesn't reset to zero
every month. Your week-1 founding clients at $250 should become $500–$1k/mo retainers by week 3.

---

## Lever 2 — Raise prices with proof

Every testimonial and case study earns you a price increase. Path:
`$250 founding → $500 starter → $1,000 growth → $2,000 scale.`
New clients pay the current rate; you grandfather early ones (or migrate them up at renewal).
**Case studies are leverage** — "I grew [client] from 2k to 18k followers in 60 days" closes the
next client at triple the price.

---

## Lever 3 — Systemize delivery (SOPs > hustle)

Write your pipeline (`04-AI-DELIVERY-PIPELINE.md`) into a **repeatable SOP**: exact steps, CapCut
template links, prompt links, naming conventions, delivery format. When delivery is a checklist,
two things happen: it's faster, and **someone else can run it.** That's the bridge to leverage.

---

## Lever 4 — Buy back your time (stay faceless, add hands)

At ~5 clients you'll be capacity-capped at 3 hrs/night. Don't raise hours — add leverage:
- Hire an **editor** on Fiverr / OnlineJobs.ph / Upwork at **$3–8/video.** Your SOP makes them
  plug-and-play. You keep the client relationship and the margin.
- Math: client pays $1,000/mo for 20 videos. Editor costs ~$100–160. You keep ~$850 and your time.
- **You become the faceless operator** — sales + quality control — not the button-pusher.
  This is how the "machine" becomes real and how you scale past your own hours.

---

## Lever 5 — Build an inbound engine (your own faceless channel)

Stop relying only on outreach. Run **your own faceless content channel** about results and short-form
tips. Post your clients' wins (with permission) as case studies. This:
- Builds the audience you started without — now as proof, not vanity.
- Generates **inbound leads** so prospects come to you pre-sold.
- Is itself a portfolio. You're demonstrating the exact service you sell.

Outbound gets you the first 5 clients. Inbound + referrals get you to 10+ without grinding DMs forever.

---

## Lever 6 — The referral flywheel

Every happy client = 1 testimonial + 1 warm intro (Day-7 script in `03-OUTREACH-SCRIPTS.md`).
Warm intros close at multiples of cold rates. Systematize it: ask at every monthly renewal.
A small referral reward (a free bonus video for any client who refers) keeps it spinning.

---

## The 90-day arc

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Target |
|-------|-------|-------|--------|
| **Prove** | 1 | Land 1–2 founding clients | $500 |
| **Convert** | 2–4 | Turn them into retainers, refine winning combo | $1.5–3k/mo |
| **Systemize** | 5–8 | SOPs, hire first editor, raise prices, start own channel | $4–6k/mo |
| **Leverage** | 9–12 | Inbound + referrals + 2nd editor, fill to 10 retainers | **$10k/mo** |

---

## The one-page operating loop (print this)

1. **Sell** — 30+ targeted DMs/day into your proven winning combo.
2. **Deliver** — batch-produce via SOP + templates (or your editor does).
3. **Retain** — pitch the retainer, track content performance, keep clients winning.
4. **Measure** — weekly review; double down on what converts, cut what doesn't.
5. **Leverage** — reinvest margin into editors + your inbound channel.
6. Repeat until 10 retainers. Then raise prices and do it again.

The system is boring on purpose. Boring, measured, and repeated is what clears $10k/month.
